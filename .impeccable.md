## Design Context

### Users
~10 internal team members at **Bhakti Sales**, a manufacturing/fulfillment company. Three tiers:
- **Admin** — full CRUD on orders + categories, sees the Activity Log and Stats.
- **Staff** — read everything; can change order status (and dispatch date) only.
- **Viewer** — read-only.

Primary context is **mobile, on the move** — the warehouse or office floor, variable lighting, often one-handed. The app is designed mobile-first at 390px (iPhone 14) and scales up. Core jobs to be done:
1. Grasp the full state of all live orders in ~3 seconds.
2. Update status or add/edit orders on the fly (admins).
3. Ask the assistant — by text or voice — questions like "what's overdue?" or "what's due in 3 days?"
4. Admins review trends (Stats) and an audit trail (Activity Log).

### Brand Personality
**Reliable, Focused, Efficient.** The interface evokes confidence and speed. It is an internal operational tool, so it prioritizes clarity over decoration — but it should still feel like a premium, thoughtfully built piece of software.

- **Product name:** OrderFlow (the live UI name). **Company:** Bhakti Sales, which owns the tool and provides the logo/watermark assets. Use "OrderFlow" in product copy; "Bhakti Sales" is the parent brand.
- **Assistant voice (chat + voice orb):** a distinct but complementary personality — **warm and friendly**. Personable and conversational with a little character and encouragement, while staying task-focused and concise. It should feel like a helpful colleague, not a corporate bot.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Bold, structured, and quietly premium.** A confident, utilitarian presentation of data — structured grid, crisp container borders, strong typographic hierarchy, and high contrast for at-a-glance reading in any lighting. Statuses are instantly recognizable via distinct color and large type.

- **Brand color:** electric indigo (OKLCH, hue 265, high chroma ~0.27). Neutrals are tinted toward indigo so even backgrounds feel on-brand without shouting.
- **Status palette** (deterministic, vivid, warehouse-legible): amber gold = Pending, cerulean = In Progress, fuchsia-violet = Packing, emerald = Dispatched. Charts on the Stats page **reuse the status palette + indigo primary** — there is no separate decorative chart palette.
- **Typeface:** Plus Jakarta Sans, on a fluid `clamp()` type scale. Never Inter / Roboto / Arial / system defaults.
- **Theme:** full light **and** dark mode, both first-class. Dark is a deep indigo-navy, not gray-black.

**Surface tiers** — *this supersedes the earlier blanket "banish glassmorphism and gradients" rule.* The product is premium & expressive, with the ornament confined to where it earns its place:
- **Data surfaces** (order lists, cards, tables, stats panels): flat, bordered, opaque, zero ornament. This is where "no decorative noise" is law.
- **Transient overlays** (drawers, bottom sheets, toasts, popovers): frosted glass via the `glass` backdrop-blur presets is allowed and encouraged to signal layering above content.
- **Expressive AI/voice zone** (chat panel, assistant avatar, voice orb): the one place gradients, glow, and richer motion are intentional — it should feel alive and premium.

**Anti-references** (what this must NOT look like): generic AI-dashboard aesthetics, gray text on colored fills, pure `#000`/`#fff`, cards nested inside cards, a large rounded icon above every heading, gradient body text.

### Design Principles
1. **Information density with clarity** — Keep mobile lists compact, but use bold typographic hierarchy so nothing feels cramped. Every interactive element is ≥ 44×44px. Zero horizontal scroll, anywhere.
2. **Deterministic, meaningful color** — Color communicates state or category, never decoration. Use vivid OKLCH for statuses and categories; desaturated, indigo-tinted neutrals for backgrounds, borders, and structure.
3. **Flat data, expressive edges** — Data surfaces stay flat and bordered; reserve frosted glass for overlays and gradient/glow/motion for the AI/voice zone. Don't let the two bleed into each other.
4. **Alive and immediate** — Realtime is the heartbeat: updates flash in seamlessly without jarring layout shifts, entrance and stagger motion is purposeful, and the voice orb makes the assistant feel present. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. **Action-oriented & role-aware** — Primary actions (the FAB, status changes, the assistant) are prominent and always within thumb reach. Surface the right capabilities per role (admin / staff / viewer) without cluttering the read-only experience.
